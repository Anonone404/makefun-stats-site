The figures people actually look up — population, the economy, prices, jobs and welfare — in one place, each with its source and year. No account, no tracking, everything on this page.

At a glance

Population

283.5 million people in 2024 — the fourth largest country in the world, and still growing by about 0.8% a year.

About 1 in 29 people on Earth lives in Indonesia. World population 8.14 billion — World Bank, 2024

More than half of all Indonesians — about 156 million people — live on Java, an island with only about 7% of the country's land. BPS, 2024

Economy

A $1.40 trillion economy in 2024 — the 16th largest in the world — growing 5.03% after 5.05% in 2023.

How Indonesia compares

Indonesia is the biggest country in Southeast Asia — by people and by economy. Here it is next to its nine ASEAN neighbours.

Indonesia has the largest population and the largest economy in Southeast Asia, but only the 5th-highest GDP per person. World Bank, 2024

Prices & jobs

Inflation cooled to 1.57% in December 2024, and unemployment fell to 4.91% in August 2024 — the lowest in the five-year series.

Welfare

Poverty has fallen every year since 2020, to 8.57% in September 2024 — and 8.47% by March 2025. Life expectancy keeps rising with it.

Poverty rate, September, percent of population — BPS, 2020–2024

September 2024's 8.57% was the lowest poverty rate since BPS began publishing the figure — BPS, Jan 2025.

Tourism

Tourism is back: 13.9 million foreign visitors in 2024, up 19.05% on 2023 — but Indonesia still ranks only 5th in Southeast Asia for arrivals.
